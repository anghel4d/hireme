`raw → unique → fresh → hard-kill → cream → strict shortlist → keepers (≤25% of strict)`

## Hard filters (binary kill)
Kill if ANY: staffing/body shop; mid-curve CRUD middleman; onsite-only outside CA with no remote; internship-tier; PM/BA/support/sales-eng dressed as eng; AI theater / prompt-only.

## Soft score 0–5: Systems depth, Agentic/AI systems, Rust/C++/low-level, Frontier/craft, Remote×Canada, Comp/leverage EV, Anti-doomer.
**Keeper bar:** mean ≥ 3.5 AND Systems ≥ 4 AND no hard kills.
**Near-keeper:** mean ≥ 3.0, Systems ≥ 3 → near_keepers only.
**Keepers ≤ 0.25 of strict shortlist** (prefer 0.12–0.20). Never lower the bar to fill a quota.
