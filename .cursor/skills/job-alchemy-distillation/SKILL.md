# Job-employer distillation (Dwemer / Observer)

Use when turning a huge employer or job bank (doomers, leftover pack, X folds, board scrapes) into high-EV keepers for Matei Anghel's hunt.

## Funnel — log counts every stage
`raw → unique → fresh → hard-kill → cream → strict shortlist → keepers`

Keepers = **≤25% of strict** (prefer 0.12–0.20). Never lower the bar to hit a quota; underfill or widen raw.

## Hard filters (binary kill)
Staffing/body shop; mid-curve CRUD middleman; onsite-only outside Canada with no remote; internship-tier; PM/BA/support/sales-eng as eng; AI theater / prompt-only.

## Soft score 0–5
Systems depth · Agentic/AI systems · Rust/C++/low-level · Frontier/craft · Remote×Canada · Comp/leverage EV · Anti-doomer.

**Keeper:** mean ≥ 3.5 AND Systems ≥ 4 AND no hard kills.
**Near-keeper:** mean ≥ 3.0, Systems ≥ 3 → park only.

## Role card (mandatory sections)
1. One-sentence takeaway
2. Why it matters for Matei (Anoptic / Observer / anolang leverage)
3. Key ideas (stack, ownership, team)
4. Caveats
5. Links — JD + careers + apply (no card without primary link)

Fields: company, title, remote, geo, stacks[], score, hard_pass:false, batch_id, see:[]

## Matei EV
Systems / agentic / Rust–C++; frontier labs and real systems seats; Canadian remote-OK. Thesis targets: Anthropic, DeepMind, SpaceXAI, Neuralink, Starfish peers, neoclouds, inference infra. Maximize life EV ≠ maximize apply count.
